# Lot 3152 — Master Landscape & Hardscape Design Specification

## 1. Project Overview & Locked-In Design Mandate

This document is the canonical architectural, botanical, and hardscape specification for **Lot 3152** (Jared & Melinda Carter). All outdated design tracks (such as S-curve beds, extruded concrete curbing, flagstone lawn steppers, 7-box garden alternates, window wells, 4-post freestanding pergolas, patio privacy walls, and AC covers/shrouds) have been removed so this specification reflects **only our current, locked-in decisions**:

1. **Maximum Open Grass (`90%–95%` Turf Preservation) Continuing Under the South Gate:** Preserve open, rectangular turf across the backyard ($679.0\text{ sq ft}$), the $4.0'\text{+}$ wide South side corridor wrap ($110.0\text{ sq ft}$), and the SE front yard ($555.0\text{ sq ft}$), with the **grass continuing directly under the South walk gate (`wx = 57.5'`) into the front yard** for seamless mowing and unified curb appeal.
2. **Straight Flush Paver Mow Strip + Prominent $45^\circ$ Chamfered Corners (Home Depot Pavestone Holland Paver):**
   * **Canonical Paver Placement Rule (Where Pavers Go vs. Where No Pavers Go):**
     * **Pavers Installed (`Grass → Mulch, Compacted Road Base, Gravel, or Home`):** Everywhere the grass meets **mulch**, **compacted road base** (including the North garden box zone and the $3.0'\times 11.0'$ rear house path), **gravel**, or **the home**, a straight flush concrete paver mowing strip is installed.
     * **NO Pavers Installed (`Grass → Driveway, Patio, or Concrete Sidewalk`):** Everywhere the grass meets the **driveway**, the **patio** ($21.0' \times 12.5'$ rear concrete patio), or a **poured concrete sidewalk** ($3.5'$ front porch sidewalk/walkway or $4.0'$ public city sidewalk), **there are NOT any pavers** (the flat poured concrete slab serves directly as the flush mower edge).
   * **Zero Curvy / S-Curve Edging:** All lawn perimeters use straight geometric runs ($\approx 215\text{ linear ft}$ total across Lot 3152).
   * **Exact Home Depot Paver to Buy (`Pavestone Holland Concrete Paver`):** Listed on Home Depot's website by exact metric mold size ($200\text{ mm} \times 100\text{ mm}$) rather than nominal "4×8":
     * **Standard 60 mm (Most Stocked in Utah):** **`7.87 in. L × 3.94 in. W × 2.36 in. H`** ($\approx \$0.68\text{–}\$0.78/\text{brick}$ in-store; $480\text{ pcs/pallet}$). Extra thickness resists shifting or tipping in Utah freeze-thaw soil.
     * **Thin 45 mm (#1 Budget Pick if In Stock Locally):** **`7.75 in. L × 4 in. W × 1.75 in. H`** (or `7.87" × 3.94" × 1.77"`, $\approx \$0.58\text{–}\$0.68/\text{brick}$; $672\text{ pcs/pallet}$). Requires only a $1.75\text{"}$ deep trench.
     * **Built-In Mower Advantages:** Features side spacer bars for automatic joint alignment and a micro-chamfered top edge so mower wheels and blades never catch on a sharp lip. *(Skip `7.63" × 3.63" × 2.25"` structural masonry concrete bricks in the building materials aisle, which lack spacer nubs/chamfers and spall in wet winter turf).*
   * **Exact Color SKUs to Buy:**
     * **Top Modern High-Contrast Pick:** **`Charcoal` / `Charcoal Blend` (Model `#21703` / HD SKU `204701612`)** or **`Antique Grey` (Model `#21778-AGR` / SKU `301595154`)** — blends seamlessly into dark brown/black mulch while creating a razor-sharp architectural frame against bright green turf and matching the black window trim/gutters and dark espresso wood stain.
     * **Top Warm Stone Match:** **`Sierra Blend` (Model `#21777` / SKU `204701614`)** or **`Old Town Blend` (Model `#15699` / `#22099`)** — mixes charcoal/pewter grey with warm taupe/tan variegation that ties directly into the `3152` front porch stacked stone wainscot and the rear tan/grey precast wall while hiding Utah dust and hard-water spots. *(Avoid `Red`, `Red Charcoal`, and `San Diego Terracotta`).*
   * **Layout & Shopping Count for Lot 3152 ($\approx 215\text{ lin ft} + 5\%$ for $45^\circ$ miter cuts):**
     * **Single Lengthwise Ribbon (`3.94"` / `~4"` wide strip):** $1.53\text{ bricks/ft} = \mathbf{345\text{ pavers}}$ (**$\approx \$240\text{–}\$270$ total**).
     * **Crosswise Soldier Course or Staggered Double Row (`7.87"` / `~8"` wide strip):** $3.05\text{ bricks/ft} = \mathbf{685\text{ pavers}}$ (**$\approx \$465\text{–}\$530$ total**).
   * **Prominent $45^\circ$ Chamfered Paver Corners:** Every $90^\circ$ turf corner is replaced by a **prominent, clearly visible $45^\circ$ diagonal chamfered paver corner** ($\approx 2.0\text{ ft} \times 2.0\text{ ft}$ to $2.5\text{ ft} \times 2.5\text{ ft}$ diagonal transition cut with $22.5^\circ$ miter joints) so the lawn mower pivots continuously around corners in a single pass without backing up or scalping.
   * **House-to-Grass & Compacted Road Base Transitions (Slab-on-Grade — Zero Basement / Zero Window Wells):** The home is slab-on-grade with **no basement and no window wells anywhere**. Along the $11.0\text{ ft}$ rear house wall (`wy = 9.0' to 20.0'`), a **$3.0\text{ ft}$ wide compacted road base sidewalk/path** (`wx = 27.0' to 30.0'`) directly connects the $21.0' \times 12.5'$ concrete patio to the $9.0'\times 40.0'$ North side compacted road base pad (`wx = 30.0'–70.0'`) and the compacted road base paths around the 5 garden boxes (`wx = 4.0'–27.0'`). Because the rear path and garden box area are compacted road base, the $6"$ flush Holland paver strip continues along the $11.0\text{ ft}$ West edge of the rear path (`wx = 26.5'–27.0', wy = 8.5'–20.0'`) with a $45^\circ$ chamfered corner into the North garden bed. Along the South stucco house wall (`wy = 41.0'`), a **$2.0\text{ ft}$ straight rock/mulch Foundation Drip Buffer** (`wy = 41.0' to 43.0'`) + $6"$ flush paver separates the stucco wall, meters, and **completely open AC condenser (no cover or shroud)** from the $4.0\text{ ft}\text{+}$ wide South side lawn corridor (`wy = 43.5' to 47.5'`).
3. **Minimalist Website Presentation Rule (`index.html`):** The live website (`index.html`) is strictly an **ultra-minimalist visual demonstration** of what we want—containing almost no prose—while all detailed architectural reconciliations, engineering rationale, plant schedules, and measurement tables live in the repository's Markdown files.

   * Run a **$1/2\text{-inch}$ brown poly drip line** down the $50.0\text{ ft}$ Western Wall bed with **$2\text{ GPH}$ pressure-compensating button emitters** ($2\text{ emitters}$ per tree + $1\text{ emitter}$ per perennial/grass).

4. **$3\text{-Inch}$ Dark Shredded Bark Mulch & $<30\text{ Min/Year}$ Maintenance:**
   * Top the entire $4.0\text{ ft} \times 50.0\text{ ft}$ ($200\text{ sq ft}$) bed with **$3\text{ inches}$ of dark espresso/black shredded bark mulch**, pulling mulch **$3\text{ inches}$ back from the base of every tree trunk** (no mulch volcanoes).
