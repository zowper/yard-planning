  1. **Architectural Framing ("A-B-A-B" Rhythm):** Instead of a monotonous "barcode" of identical evergreens, the **4 upright evergreen junipers (`J1`–`J4`) sit directly in front of the 4 interior concrete wall columns ($wy = 10.0', 20.0', 30.0', 40.0'$) like living green pillars**, while the **5 lush, soft-leaved columnar trees (`S1`–`S5`) sit centered inside each of the 5 stone wall panels ($wy = 5.0', 15.0', 25.0', 35.0', 45.0'$)** like framed artwork.

Native to Utah (*Amelanchier alnifolia*), it thrives in heavy alkaline clay and reflected wall heat, bursts with **snow-white blossoms in April** for Melinda, fills out in **ultra-lush dark emerald-green round leaves all summer**, produces sweet **edible blueberry-like Juneberries**, and turns **fiery red-orange in autumn**.

    * **Size & Habit:** $20\text{–}25\text{ ft}$ tall $\times$ **$5.0\text{–}6.0\text{ ft}$ wide** ($\approx \$60\text{–}\$90$ in `#5` pots).
    * **Advantage:** Grows $2.5\text{ ft/year}$ with ultra-dense emerald-green leaves that shimmer in the breeze and golden-yellow fall color; specifically bred for alkaline soil and heat/drought tolerance.
  * *(Bonus Compact/Budget Option: **`'Fine Line'` Columnar Buckthorn**, *Frangula alnus 'Ron Williams'*, $10\text{–}12\text{ ft H} \times 2.5\text{–}3.0\text{ ft W}$, $\approx \$30\text{–}\$45$ each—soft fern-like lush green foliage that never needs pruning).*

* **Part 2 — 4× Upright Evergreen Junipers Aligned with the 4 Interior Wall Columns (`J1`–`J4` at $wy = 10.0', 20.0', 30.0', 40.0'$, $wx = 1.75'$):**

### 2.4 Soil, Microclimate & Planting Instructions Next to the Precast Concrete Wall
1. **Exact Trunk Offset from the Cement Wall ($wx = 1.75'\text{–}2.0'$):** Dig all tree holes with the **trunk centerline at $wx = 2.0\text{ ft}$** ($24\text{ in}$ East of the wall for the 5 leafy panel pillars, and $wx = 1.75\text{ ft}$ / $21\text{ in}$ East of the wall for the 4 column junipers) so back branches have breathing room against reflected heat while front foliage stays inside the $wx = 4.0\text{ ft}$ bed line.
2. **Crowned Bed Grading (`+3" to +4"` in `2027 Phase 2`) & Elevated Root-Flare Planting (`+2" to +3"` in `2028 Phase 3` — "High & Wide" Rule for Utah Alkaline Clay, $\text{pH } 7.5\text{–}8.2$):** Because October 2026 heavy-rainfall site photos showed water pooling along the settled West precast wall trench (`wx = 0.0'–4.5'`), first backfill and **crown the $4.0\text{ ft}$ West Wall Bed $+3"\text{ to }+4"$ higher than the flush Holland paver strip (`wx = 4.0'–4.5'`)** during `2027 Phase 2` topsoil grading. Then in `2028 Phase 3`, dig each planting hole **$2\times$ as wide as the nursery pot** ($24"\text{–}30"$ wide) and **slightly shallower** so the **top of every tree and juniper root flare sits $+2\text{ to }+3\text{ inches}$ ABOVE the crowned bed grade** (preventing *Phytophthora* root rot; see [`docs/04-irrigation-and-drainage.md`](./04-irrigation-and-drainage.md)). Backfill with **$80\%$ native clay soil mixed with $20\%$ organic compost or aged pine bark fines** (never $100\%$ potting mix, which creates a "bathtub effect").
3. **$3\text{-Inch}$ Dark Shredded Bark Mulch & $<30\text{ Min/Year}$ Maintenance:** Top the $200\text{ sq ft}$ bed with $3"$ of dark espresso/black shredded bark mulch (pulled $3"$ back from trunks). Once a year in **early March**, cut dried *Karl Foerster* grasses and *Catmint/Russian Sage* down to $6"$ above ground ($\approx 20\text{ min}$ total).

* **Mature Dimensions:** $30'\text{–}35'\text{ H} \times 20'\text{–}25'\text{ W}$ label spec (**typically $28'\text{–}32'\text{ H}$ in Utah alkaline clay**, $\approx 1.5'\text{–}2.0'\text{/yr}$ growth rate; $10.0'\text{–}12.5'$ mature canopy radius).

  * **$10.5\text{ ft}$ North** to the concrete driveway (`wy = 29.0'`).

### 3.2 Lot 3152 House Elevation Benchmarks vs. High-Canopy Pruning (`8.0'–9.5'` Trunk Attachment $\rightarrow$ `11'–12'+` Outer Lawn Ceiling)
* **House Elevation Benchmarks:**
  * Top of `'3152'` front porch stone pedestal base: $\approx 3.5\text{ ft}$
  * 1st-floor covered front porch roof eave / gutter line: $\approx 9.5\text{ to }10.0\text{ ft}$
  * 2nd-story bedroom & front windows (sill to head): $\approx 12.5\text{ ft}$ to $17.0\text{ ft}$
  * 2nd-story main roof eaves: $\approx 18.5\text{ to }19.5\text{ ft}$
  * Top peak of 2nd-story white shake garage gable ridge: $\approx 25.0\text{ to }26.0\text{ ft}$
  * Mature Norwegian Sunset® Maple apex ($28'\text{–}32'\text{+}$ in Utah): Sits cleanly **$4\text{ to }8\text{ ft}$ above the highest roof peak** in 12–15 years.
* **Why We Avoid a `15.0 ft` Bare Trunk:** Stripping the lower $15.0\text{ ft}$ of trunk on a $30\text{ ft}$ tree violates the **ISA `1/3` Clear-Trunk Rule ($67\%$ live crown ratio)**—turning $50\%$ of the tree into a bare "broccoli-on-a-toothpick" pole whose canopy doesn't start until halfway up the 2nd-story bedroom windows, exposing $15'$ of thin maple bark to **Utah Southwest winter sunscald**, and starving lower trunk taper.
* **The Architectural Sweet Spot (`8.0'–9.5'` Trunk Attachment $\rightarrow$ `11.0'–12.0'+` Outer Lawn Skirt):** Aligning the lowest permanent scaffold branch attachment on the central trunk at **$8.0\text{ to }9.5\text{ ft}$** matches the exact horizontal plane of the **`'3152'` covered front porch roof eave ($\approx 9.5'\text{–}10.0'$)**. Because Norwegian Sunset® Maple branches sweep **upward at a $45^\circ$ ascending angle**, branches attaching at $8.5'\text{–}9.2'$ on the trunk naturally rise to **$11.0\text{ to }12.0\text{+}\text{ ft}$ high out over the lawn**!
* **3-Stage Multi-Year Crown-Raising Schedule:**
  1. **Stage 1 — At Planting (`2027`, Tree is `10'–12' H`):** Leave temporary lower branches starting at **$5.0\text{ to }5.5\text{ ft}$** so they feed sugars to thicken the lower trunk flare and shade the young bark against sunscald.
  2. **Stage 2 — Early Establishment (`Years 2–4` [2029–2030], Tree is `15'–18' H`):** In late winter (dormant season), remove the lowest temporary tier (never removing $>20\%\text{–}25\%$ of live crown in a single year) to establish a **$6.8\text{ to }7.5\text{ ft}$ clear trunk** for effortless walk-under mowing.
  3. **Stage 3 — Mature Architectural Form (`Years 5–8+` [2031–2033+], Tree is `22'–32' H`):** Raise the permanent lowest scaffold attachment to **$8.0\text{ to }9.5\text{ ft}$ on the trunk** (level with the front porch eave), prune off drooping underside twigs to lock in the **$11.0'\text{–}12.0'\text{+}$ outer lawn clearance ceiling**, and maintain **$15\%$ airy interior crown thinning**.

## 4. Melinda's Fragrant Hyacinth Border, Continuous $2.0\text{ ft}$ South Bed & Utility Box Screen
