DECISION 1 · OVERALL STYLE

Primary Landscape Track: Maximum Lawn vs. Wide S-Curve Beds

Applies to: Backyard & Front Yard
RECOMMENDED · TRACK B 90%–95% Open Lawn
Maximum-Grass / Zero-Footprint Design

Preserves almost every square foot of lawn for kids, play, and easy straight-line mowing. Visual interest shifts upward to the fence and corners:

  • Soil-Flush Concrete Mowing Strips: Zero trimming required; mower wheels roll right along the edge.
  • Soil-Flush Flagstone Steppers: Set flush in the turf from patio to garden boxes so the mower drives right over them.
  • Vertical Fence Accents: Cedar slat/black metal trellis panels mounted directly on the white vinyl fence.
  • Corner Pocket Tree: Single 4'–5' radius curved corner bed for a focal tree + warm 2700K uplighting.
ALTERNATIVE · TRACK A Traditional Curbed Beds
Lush S-Curve Beds & Dedicated Gravel Garden Pad

Uses 6-inch raised stamped concrete curbing to carve out deeper, flowing S-curve perennial beds and a dedicated crushed gravel zone around the garden boxes:

  • 6" Raised Concrete Curbing: Sweeping S-curves along the perimeter fences and back wall.
  • Crushed Granite Garden Pad: All 7 raised cedar garden beds sit on a weed-barrier gravel pad (no mowing between boxes).
  • Front Lawn Flagstone Walkway: 4' wide curving flagstone path cutting across the front grass from driveway to porch.
DECISION 2 · NW BACKYARD GARDEN ZONE

Raised Garden Bed Layout (7 Cedar Boxes @ 6.0' × 3.0' = 126 sq ft)

Test all 3 layouts live in the CAD Blueprint above!
RECOMMENDED · OPTION B 10.25' Gate Clear
"Gate-Clear Utah" (2 Patio + 4 N. Fence + 1 W. Wall)

Keeps 2 boxes North of the patio (4.5' clear of basement window well) and 4 boxes along the North fence, moving the 5th North box onto the sunny West precast wall. Opens a full 10.25' wide turnaround at the NW house corner so trailers, ATVs, or wheelbarrows can drive straight through the North vehicle gate!

OPTION A · ORIGINAL 5.5' Corner Pinch
"Your Utah" (2 Patio + 5 North Fence)

Places 2 N–S boxes North of the patio and 5 N–S boxes along the North fence (1.75' gaps). Preserves the entire West wall for trees/shrubs, but Box N5 extends to X=24.5', leaving a 5.5' diagonal pinch point at the NW house corner for vehicles entering the North gate.

OPTION C · PERIMETER L 100% Full Sun
"Sunny NW Wall L" (4 N. Fence + 3 W. Wall)

Hugs all 7 boxes along the NW perimeter walls (4 on North fence, 3 on West wall) in 100% unobstructed southern/western sun. Leaves the entire area North of the patio (20' × 12.5') completely open for lawn, play, and vehicle access.

DECISION 3 · FIRE FEATURE

Propane Fire Pit Placement: Melinda's Patio Lounge vs. Yard Corner

21.0' × 12.5' Existing Concrete Patio (262.5 sq ft)
RECOMMENDED · MELINDA'S IDEA 0 sq ft Grass Lost
On Existing Concrete Patio (2-Zone Split)

Divides the 21.0' patio into Zone 1 (9.5' × 12.5' Grill & Dining near sliding door) and Zone 2 (11.5' × 12.5' Fire Pit Lounge). Uses a rectangular propane fire table (28"×48" or 32"×52") with a concealed 20-lb tank and flush metal burner cover so it doubles as an everyday coffee table. Zero hardscape cost & zero lawn lost!

OPTION B · HYBRID +64–80 sq ft Pavers
Connected Patio Corner Bump-Out

Pours or lays an 8'×8' or 10' curved paver extension directly off the outer corner of the existing 12.5'×21' slab. Keeps the fire pit dry and step-free right off the patio while freeing the main 262.5 sq ft slab for a larger dining table + sectional.

OPTION C · YARD CORNER -150 sq ft Lawn
Dedicated Backyard Corner Pad

Builds a separate 13.5' diameter gravel/paver circle in the SW backyard corner. Requires placing the burner center 9.5' out from the vinyl fence to protect vinyl panels from heat and seat 4–6 chairs, consuming ~150 sq ft of prime lawn.

DECISION 4 · FENCING & GATES

Side-Yard White Vinyl Fence Setbacks & Gate Positions

145.5 Linear Feet Total White Vinyl Privacy Fence
RECOMMENDED · STUCCO SETBACK N: X=70.0' | S: X=57.5'
Relative Symmetry Behind Stone Returns (Encloses Meters)
  • North Side (Garage): 8.0'–8.5' Double-Swing Vehicle Gate at X=70.0' (set back 3.2' behind NE garage face, cleanly behind the 2.0' stone masonry wrap & downspout). Encloses 360.0 sq ft.
  • South Side (Porch): 4.0' Single-Swing Pedestrian Gate at X=57.5' (2.2' behind SE stucco corner / 8.2' behind porch pillar). Hides gas & electric meters behind the gate while framing the front stone pillar!
OPTION B · SE CORNER ALIGNMENT N: X=70.0' | S: X=59.7'
South Gate Flush with SE Stucco Corner (X=59.7')
  • North Side: Same X=70.0' Double Vehicle Gate position (3.2' setback).
  • South Side: Pushes the South pedestrian gate 2.2' further East to align flush with the SE house stucco corner (6.0' behind the front porch column), gaining 19.8 sq ft of enclosed side yard.
DECISION 5 · SIDE YARD TREATMENTS

North & South Side Yard Surfacing, Walkway & Utility Screening

North: 9.0' × 43.2' · South: 9.0' × 29.7'
RECOMMENDED · FUNCTIONAL SPLIT Matches Side Yard Render
South Flagstone Path + AC Screen & North Utility/Trailer Pad
  • South Side Yard (Human Gate): Large charcoal flagstone steppers inlaid in grey crushed granite gravel, shade plantings (hostas, ferns, ornamental grasses) along the house wall, and a horizontal cedar-slat screen hiding the 3'×3' AC unit while preserving a 6.0' walk path.
  • North Side Yard (Vehicle Gate): Compacted crushed stone/road-base utility pad over commercial weed barrier from the 8.5' double gate to the backyard for trash bins, trailer/mower parking, and clean access to the water spigot (X=51') and raised garden beds.
OPTION B · POURED CONCRETE RV/UTILITY PAD Heavy-Duty Hardscape
Poured Concrete North Pad + Gravel/Stepper South Corridor

Pours a dedicated 4" concrete slab down the 9.0' North side yard for rolling heavy bins, trailers, or utility carts, paired with decorative gravel and flagstone steppers on the South side corridor.

DECISION 6 · FRONT YARD & PARK STRIPS

Front Yard (813.5 sq ft), Utility Box Camouflage & 6.0' Park Strips

555 sq ft SE Front + 258.5 sq ft NE Strip + 6.0' Deep Park Strips
RECOMMENDED · WATER-WISE STRIPS + FULL LAWN Syracuse / Utah Friendly
Open Front Lawn + Utility Box Screen + Water-Wise 6' Park Strips
  • SE Front Lawn (555 sq ft): Continuous lush turf with soil-flush concrete mowing strips; focal specimen tree (*Japanese Maple* or *Little Gem Magnolia*) planted inside the existing foundation bed corner with lavender & boxwoods.
  • Green Utility Box (X=90', Y=39.5'): Screened on 3 sides with upright *Karl Foerster* feather reed grasses inside the front-left bed (leaving the front service face accessible).
  • NE Front Strip (258.5 sq ft): Clean decorative rock or turf transition leading up to the 8.5' North vehicle gate.
  • 6.0' Park Strips (South 21' & North 11'): Drip-irrigated decorative cobble/gravel with accent boulders and drought-hardy perennials/ornamental grasses (no narrow turf strips to edge or overspray onto the sidewalk/street).
OPTION B · TRADITIONAL WALKWAY & SOD Track A Front Design
Mid-Lawn Flagstone Walkway + Sod/Tree Park Strips

Adds a 4.0' wide curving natural flagstone walkway from the driveway across the SE front lawn to the front porch, plants a specimen tree in a curbed center-lawn mulch ring, and finishes the 6.0' park strips in lawn/street trees.
