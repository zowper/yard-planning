## 🎯 Target Design Summary (What We Want)

1. **Maximum Open Grass (Track B):** Preserve $90\%\text{–}95\%$ of open turf across the backyard and front yard for recreation and effortless mowing.
2. **Straight Flush Paver Mow Strip + $45^\circ$ Chamfered Corners (Home Depot Pavestone Holland Paver):** **Zero curvy / S-curve edging.** Continuous soil-flush paver mowing strip ($\approx 195\text{ linear ft}$ total: $120\text{ ft}$ Backyard + $75\text{ ft}$ Front Yard) around all bed perimeters with **$45^\circ$ angled corners** ($2.0\text{ ft} \times 2.0\text{ ft}$ with $22.5^\circ$ miter cuts) so the mower rides directly on the pavers and turns in one pass:
   - **Exact Paver to Buy:** **Pavestone Holland Concrete Paver** — listed on Home Depot's website by exact metric mold size as **`7.87 in. L × 3.94 in. W × 2.36 in. H (60 mm)`** ($\approx \$0.68\text{–}\$0.78/\text{brick}$ in-store) or **`7.75 in. × 4 in. × 1.75 in. (45 mm)`** ($\approx \$0.58\text{–}\$0.68/\text{brick}$). *(Skip `7.63" × 3.63"` structural concrete bricks, which lack spacer nubs/beveled edges and spall in winter freeze-thaw).*
   - **Exact Colors to Buy:** **`Charcoal` / `Charcoal Blend` (`#21703` / SKU `204701612`)** or **`Antique Grey` (`#21778-AGR`)** for a crisp modern frame between dark mulch and green turf that matches the black trim and dark espresso wood; or **`Sierra Blend` (`#21777` / SKU `204701614`)** / **`Old Town Blend` (`#15699` / `#22099`)** for warm grey/charcoal/tan marbling that matches the `3152` front stone wainscot and West precast wall.
   - **Quantity & Layout Options (~195 lin ft + 5% cuts):** **Single Lengthwise Row (`~4"` wide strip)** = **`315 bricks` (~$220–$245)**; or **Crosswise / Staggered Double Row (`~8"` wide strip)** = **`625 bricks` (~$430–$485)**. Flat poured concrete slabs (patio, porch walk, driveway, sidewalk) serve directly as flush mower edges without redundant pavers.
3. **$2.0\text{ ft}$ Foundation Rock/Mulch Drip Buffer:** Separates vertical stucco house walls (and the $4.0\text{ ft} \times 1.3\text{ ft}$ rear basement egress window well) from the flush paver strip and lawn irrigation.
4. **7 Raised Cedar Garden Beds ($6.0\text{ ft} \times 3.0\text{ ft} = 126\text{ sq ft}$):** Oriented N–S in the sunny NW backyard zone (`Gate-Clear Utah`: 2 North of Patio + 4 North Fence + 1 West Wall for a $10.25\text{ ft}$ vehicle gate turnaround, or `2 Patio + 5 North Fence` / `4 North + 3 West`).
5. **Patio Propane Fire Table Lounge:** Rectangular propane fire table with flush burner cover centered on the existing $21.0\text{ ft} \times 12.5\text{ ft}$ concrete patio under the dark espresso timber pergola ($0\text{ sq ft}$ of lawn lost).
6. **White Vinyl Fence & Stucco-Setback Gates ($145.5\text{ lin ft}$):**
   - **North Vehicle Gate ($wx = 70.0\text{ ft}$):** $8.5\text{ ft}$ double-swing gate set back $3.2\text{ ft}$ West of NE garage face ($1.2\text{ ft}$ behind the $2.0\text{ ft}$ stone wrap), leading to a compacted gravel utility/trailer pad.
   - **South Pedestrian Gate ($wx = 57.5\text{ ft}$):** $4.0\text{ ft}$ walk gate set back $2.2\text{ ft}$ West of SE stucco corner (enclosing the gas/electric meters at $wx = 51.0\text{ ft}\text{–}54.5\text{ ft}$), leading to a flagstone & gravel path with a horizontal cedar AC screen.
7. **Front Yard & Water-Wise $6.0\text{ ft}$ Park Strips:** Unbroken $555\text{ sq ft}$ SE front lawn, 3-sided ornamental grass screen around the green utility box, specimen tree inside the foundation bed, and drip-irrigated rock/boulder/grass $6.0\text{ ft}$ street park strips.
