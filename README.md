## 🎯 Locked-In Master Design Decisions (What We Are Building)

1. **Maximum Open Grass:** Preserve $90\%\text{–}95\%$ of open turf across the backyard ($679\text{ sq ft}$), the $4.0'\text{+}$ wide South side corridor wrap ($110\text{ sq ft}$), and the SE front yard ($555\text{ sq ft}$) for recreation and effortless mowing.
2. **Straight Flush Paver Mow Strip + Prominent $45^\circ$ Chamfered Corners (Home Depot Pavestone Holland Paver):** **Zero curvy / S-curve edging.** Continuous soil-flush paver mowing strip ($\approx 195\text{ linear ft}$ total: $120\text{ ft}$ Backyard + $75\text{ ft}$ Front Yard) with **clearly visible, prominent $45^\circ$ diagonal chamfered paver corners** ($\approx 2.0\text{ ft} \times 2.0\text{ ft}$ to $2.5\text{ ft} \times 2.5\text{ ft}$ diagonal cuts with $22.5^\circ$ miter joints) so the mower rides directly on the pavers and pivots at $45^\circ$ in one pass:
   - **Where Pavers Go vs. Where No Pavers Go (Master Rule):**
     - **Pavers ARE Installed:** Everywhere **the grass meets mulch, gravel, or the home** (along the $4.0'$ West wall mulch bed, the $2.0'$ South fence mulch bed, the $8.0'$ North garden box gravel zone, the $2.0'$ South house wall/AC buffer & home foundation transitions, and the $2.0'$ Front Yard mulch beds).
     - **NO Pavers Are Installed:** Everywhere **the grass meets the driveway, a patio, a sidewalk, or the cement pad** (along the $18.0'\times 23.5'$ concrete driveway, the $21.0'\times 12.5'$ backyard concrete patio, the $3.0'\times 11.0'$ rear house concrete sidewalk, the $3.5'$ front porch concrete sidewalk/walkway, the $4.0'$ city concrete sidewalk, and the $9.0'\times 43.2'$ North side cement pad—all of which act directly as flat concrete flush mower edges without pavers).
   - **Exact Paver to Buy:** **Pavestone Holland Concrete Paver** — listed on Home Depot's website by exact metric mold size as **`7.87 in. L × 3.94 in. W × 2.36 in. H (60 mm)`** ($\approx \$0.68\text{–}\$0.78/\text{brick}$ in-store) or **`7.75 in. × 4 in. × 1.75 in. (45 mm)`** ($\approx \$0.58\text{–}\$0.68/\text{brick}$). *(Skip `7.63" × 3.63"` structural concrete bricks, which lack spacer nubs/beveled edges and spall in winter freeze-thaw).*
   - **Exact Colors to Buy:** **`Charcoal` / `Charcoal Blend` (`#21703` / SKU `204701612`)** or **`Antique Grey` (`#21778-AGR`)** for a crisp modern frame between dark mulch and green turf that matches the black trim and dark espresso wood; or **`Sierra Blend` (`#21777` / SKU `204701614`)** / **`Old Town Blend` (`#15699` / `#22099`)** for warm grey/charcoal/tan marbling that matches the `3152` front stone wainscot and West precast wall.
   - **Quantity & Layout Options (~195 lin ft + 5% cuts):** **Single Lengthwise Row (`~4"` wide strip)** = **`315 bricks` (~$220–$245)**; or **Crosswise / Staggered Double Row (`~8"` wide strip)** = **`625 bricks` (~$430–$485)**.
3. **Western Back Wall Bed (`4.0 ft` Deep + `6"` Paver = `4.5 ft` to Grass) — The `5 + 4` Alternating Designer Hybrid + Under-Story Ribbon:**
   - **Locked-In `5 + 4` Designer Hybrid (`~$280–$380` total tree cost):** **5× Leafy Columnar Pillars** (`S1`–`S5` centered inside each `10.0 ft` precast wall panel at $wy = 5.0', 15.0', 25.0', 35.0', 45.0'$, $wx = 2.0'$: **#1 `'Standing Ovation'` Serviceberry** $15'\text{ H} \times 4.0'\text{ W}$, **#2 `'Crimson Spire'` Columnar Oak** $20'\text{–}25'\text{ H} \times 5'\text{–}6'\text{ W}$ with marcescent winter leaves, or **#3 `'Parkland Pillar'` Birch** $20'\text{–}25'\text{ H} \times 5'\text{–}6'\text{ W}$) alternating with **4× Upright Evergreen Junipers** (`J1`–`J4` aligned directly in front of the 4 interior concrete wall columns at $wy = 10.0', 20.0', 30.0', 40.0'$, $wx = 1.75'$: **`$20` Home Depot Upright Junipers** or **`'Taylor'` Junipers** $18'\text{–}25'\text{ H} \times 3.0'\text{–}3.5'\text{ W}$). Blocks the City Works buildings/antennas and casts afternoon shade with **zero claustrophobia** in the $17.5\text{ ft}$ patio-to-wall span.
   - **Under-Story Planting Beneath & Between the Trees ($wx = 2.95'$):** 10-spot alternating ribbon ($wy = 2.3', 7.6', 12.4', 17.6', 22.4', 27.6', 32.4', 37.6', 42.4', 47.4'$) of **5× Purple Pollinator Perennials** (**`'Walker's Low'` or `'Cat's Pajamas'` Catmint**, **`'Little Spire'` Russian Sage**, or **`'Hidcote'` English Lavender** + **5× Coneflower**) and **5× `'Karl Foerster'` Feather Reed Grasses** ($3'\text{–}5'\text{ H}$ vertical golden plumes).
4. **$3.0\text{ ft}$ Rear House Concrete Sidewalk (Patio to North Cement Pad) & $2.0\text{ ft}$ South Wall Buffer:** A **$3.0\text{ ft}$ wide poured concrete sidewalk** ($3.0'\text{ W} \times 11.0'\text{ L} = 33.0\text{ sq ft}$, $wx = 27.0'\text{–}30.0'$, $wy = 9.0'\text{–}20.0'$) runs directly against the rear house foundation wall between the $21.0'\times 12.5'$ concrete patio and the $9.0'\times 43.2'$ North side cement pad (the home is slab-on-grade with **no basement and no window wells anywhere**). Where the grass meets this $3.0\text{ ft}$ rear sidewalk, the patio, or the North cement pad, there are **no pavers**. Along the South house wall, a $2.0\text{ ft}$ drip/utility buffer + horizontal cedar AC screen + $6"$ flush paver separates the home's stucco, meters, and AC unit from the $4.0'\text{+}$ wide South side lawn corridor.
5. **5 Raised Cedar Garden Boxes ($3.0\text{ ft}\text{ W} \times 6.0\text{ ft}\text{ L} = 90\text{ sq ft}$):** Started as an in-ground flat-dirt garden plot in 2027 (Phase 2) and upgraded in 2030 (Phase 5) to **5 Western Red Cedar garden boxes** (`N1`–`N5`), each $3.0\text{ ft}$ wide (East–West) by $6.0\text{ ft}$ long (North–South), running **North-to-South** in a single row along the North backyard white vinyl fence ($1.0\text{ ft}$ N fence gap + $6.0\text{ ft}$ N–S box + $1.0\text{ ft}$ S gravel gap + $6"$ flush paver where grass meets gravel = **$8.5\text{ ft}$ total from North fence to grass**).
6. **House-Attached 2-Pillar Solid-Roof Heavy-Timber Patio Awning (`Option #1C`) & Bistro Lights + L-Shaped Patio Furniture + Rectangular Propane Fire Table:** Every view of the $21.0\text{ ft} \times 12.5\text{ ft}$ ($262.5\text{ sq ft}$) concrete patio features a **house-attached (ledger-mounted) dark-espresso stained heavy-timber awning** with **strictly TWO pillars ($6\times 6$ or $8\times 8$) on the outer West corners of the patio (`wx = 18.0', wy = 20.5'` & `40.5'`) and ZERO pillars against the home** (attached directly to the 2nd-floor rim joist ledger at `wx = 30.0'` above the sliding glass door and living room window). Built with a clear-span $21.0\text{ ft}$ Glulam / steel-reinforced West header beam, $45^\circ$ corner knee braces, matte-black `Simpson Outdoor Accents` steel hardware, East–West $2\times 8$ rafters (`18" O.C.`), a **$100\%$ opaque $1\times 6$ Tongue-and-Groove cedar wood ceiling deck underneath**, a **sleek matte-black standing-seam architectural metal roof & front rain gutter on top** (zero translucent plastic—never yellows or shows dirt from underneath, while protecting furniture $24/7/365$), and **warm $2700\text{K}$ overhead bistro string lights**, sheltering **L-shaped outdoor sectional furniture** and a **long rectangular dual-purpose propane fire table** (acts as both a table and a long propane fire pit, preserving $100\%$ of the lawn).
7. **Narrow $9.0\text{ ft}$ Side Yards, Stucco-Setback Gates ($145.5\text{ lin ft}$), Neighbor Property Line Contrast & $50/50$ Neighbor Fence Cost Split:** Both side yards are a narrow **$9.0\text{ ft}$ wide**, and neighboring lots use distinct ground surfaces so property lines are immediately visible:
   - **Fence & Gate Cost-Split & Phasing Rule:** We **split the cost of the shared property-line vinyl fences $50/50$ with our neighbors** ($57.5\text{ lin ft}$ South fence in **2026** and $70.0\text{ lin ft}$ North fence in **2027**), while **we pay $100\%$ for our own gates and house-side returns** ($4.0\text{ ft}$ South walk gate + $5.0\text{ ft}$ return wings in **2026**, and $8.5\text{ ft}$ North double-swing vehicle gate in **2027**).
   - **Neighbor Property Line Contrast:** **North Neighbor (`wy < 0.0'`): Completely Empty (Simply Bare Dirt)** outside our North fence/gate line; **South Neighbor (`wy > 50.0'`): White Rocks** outside our South fence/bed line.
   - **North Side Yard ($9.0'\text{ W} \times 43.2'\text{ L}$) & Vehicle Gate ($wx = 70.0\text{ ft}$ — Installed 2027):** Full poured concrete (cement) utility/trailer pad ($388.8\text{ sq ft}$, poured 2028) connected directly to the rear patio via the $3.0'$ rear-wall concrete sidewalk, with a $70.0\text{ ft}$ North white vinyl fence ($50\%$ neighbor split) and an $8.5\text{ ft}$ double-swing white vinyl vehicle gate ($100\%$ our cost) installed in **2027** and set back $3.2\text{ ft}$ West of the NE garage face ($1.2\text{ ft}$ behind the $2.0\text{ ft}$ stone wrap).
   - **South Side Yard ($9.0'\text{ W} \times 29.7'\text{ L}$), $4.0'\text{+}$ Grass Wrap & Pedestrian Gate ($wx = 57.5\text{ ft}$ — Installed 2026):** The backyard lawn wraps **$4.0\text{ ft}\text{+}$ wide** (`wy = 43.5'–47.5'`) all the way down the $9.0\text{ ft}$ South side corridor (with flush pavers on both sides where the grass meets the $2.0'$ South fence mulch bed and the $2.0'$ home wall/AC buffer) so it looks like continuous green grass going down the entire South side to the $57.5\text{ ft}$ South white vinyl fence ($50\%$ neighbor split) and $4.0\text{ ft}$ single-swing walk gate at $wx = 57.5\text{ ft}$ ($100\%$ our cost, installed in **2026** and set back $2.2\text{ ft}$ West of the SE stucco corner, enclosing the gas/electric meters and AC unit).
8. **Front Yard & Water-Wise $6.0\text{ ft}$ Park Strips:** Unbroken $555\text{ sq ft}$ SE front lawn with $6"$ flush Holland pavers and **prominent $45^\circ$ chamfered corners** where the grass meets the $2.0\text{ ft}$ mulch beds or home (and **no pavers** where the grass meets the driveway, porch sidewalk/walkway, or city sidewalk), 3-sided ornamental grass screen around the green utility box, specimen tree inside the front porch bed, and drip-irrigated rock/boulder/grass $6.0\text{ ft}$ street park strips.
