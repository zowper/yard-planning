## 🎯 Target Design Summary (What We Want)

1. **Maximum Open Grass (Track B):** Preserve $90\%\text{–}95\%$ of open turf across the backyard and front yard for recreation and effortless mowing.
2. **Straight Flush Paver Mow Strip + Prominent $45^\circ$ Chamfered Corners (Home Depot Pavestone Holland Paver):** **Zero curvy / S-curve edging.** Continuous soil-flush paver mowing strip ($\approx 195\text{ linear ft}$ total: $120\text{ ft}$ Backyard + $75\text{ ft}$ Front Yard) around all bed perimeters with **clearly visible, prominent $45^\circ$ diagonal chamfered paver corners** ($\approx 2.0\text{ ft} \times 2.0\text{ ft}$ to $2.5\text{ ft} \times 2.5\text{ ft}$ diagonal cuts with $22.5^\circ$ miter joints) so the mower rides directly on the pavers and pivots at $45^\circ$ in one pass:
   - **Exact Paver to Buy:** **Pavestone Holland Concrete Paver** — listed on Home Depot's website by exact metric mold size as **`7.87 in. L × 3.94 in. W × 2.36 in. H (60 mm)`** ($\approx \$0.68\text{–}\$0.78/\text{brick}$ in-store) or **`7.75 in. × 4 in. × 1.75 in. (45 mm)`** ($\approx \$0.58\text{–}\$0.68/\text{brick}$). *(Skip `7.63" × 3.63"` structural concrete bricks, which lack spacer nubs/beveled edges and spall in winter freeze-thaw).*
   - **Exact Colors to Buy:** **`Charcoal` / `Charcoal Blend` (`#21703` / SKU `204701612`)** or **`Antique Grey` (`#21778-AGR`)** for a crisp modern frame between dark mulch and green turf that matches the black trim and dark espresso wood; or **`Sierra Blend` (`#21777` / SKU `204701614`)** / **`Old Town Blend` (`#15699` / `#22099`)** for warm grey/charcoal/tan marbling that matches the `3152` front stone wainscot and West precast wall.
   - **Quantity & Layout Options (~195 lin ft + 5% cuts):** **Single Lengthwise Row (`~4"` wide strip)** = **`315 bricks` (~$220–$245)**; or **Crosswise / Staggered Double Row (`~8"` wide strip)** = **`625 bricks` (~$430–$485)**. Flat poured concrete slabs (patio, $3.0'$ rear sidewalk, porch walk, driveway, sidewalk) serve directly as flush mower edges without redundant pavers.
3. **10 Juniper Trees Along the $50.0\text{ ft}$ West Back Wall (Evenly Spaced — Two Per Panel):** Inside the $4.0\text{ ft}$ deep dark-mulched West precast wall bed ($+6"$ flush paver $= 4.5\text{ ft}$ to grass), plant **10 upright green columnar Juniper trees (`'Taylor'` or `'Spartan'` Juniper) evenly spaced 2 per $10.0\text{ ft}$ precast wall panel (@ $5.0\text{ ft}$ O.C.)** (`J1`–`J10` at $wy = 2.5', 7.5', 12.5', 17.5', 22.5', 27.5', 32.5', 37.5', 42.5', 47.5'$), accented by *Little Spire* Russian sage/lavender and coneflower in the center of each panel and *Karl Foerster* feather reed grasses at the 4 interior columns.
4. **$3.0\text{ ft}$ Rear House Concrete Sidewalk (Patio to North Cement Pad) & $2.0\text{ ft}$ South Wall Buffer:** A **$3.0\text{ ft}$ wide poured concrete sidewalk** ($3.0'\text{ W} \times 11.0'\text{ L} = 33.0\text{ sq ft}$, $wx = 27.0'\text{–}30.0'$, $wy = 9.0'\text{–}20.0'$) runs directly against the rear house foundation wall between the $21.0'\times 12.5'$ concrete patio and the $9.0'\times 43.2'$ North side cement pad (note: the home is slab-on-grade with **no basement and no window wells anywhere**). Along the South house wall, a $2.0\text{ ft}$ drip/utility buffer separates the stucco, meters, and AC unit from the $4.0'\text{+}$ wide South side lawn corridor.
5. **5 Raised Cedar Garden Boxes ($3.0\text{ ft}\text{ W} \times 6.0\text{ ft}\text{ L} = 90\text{ sq ft}$):** Exactly **5 garden boxes** (`N1`–`N5`), each $3.0\text{ ft}$ wide (East–West) by $6.0\text{ ft}$ long (North–South), running **North-to-South** in a single row along the North backyard white vinyl fence ($1.0\text{ ft}$ N fence gap + $6.0\text{ ft}$ N–S box + $1.0\text{ ft}$ S gap + $6"$ flush paver = **$8.5\text{ ft}$ total from North fence to grass**).
6. **Patio Timber Awning & Bistro Lights + L-Shaped Patio Furniture + Rectangular Propane Fire Table:** Every view of the $21.0\text{ ft} \times 12.5\text{ ft}$ ($262.5\text{ sq ft}$) concrete patio features the **dark-espresso stained timber awning/pergola with warm $2700\text{K}$ overhead bistro string lights**, **L-shaped outdoor sectional furniture**, and a **long rectangular dual-purpose propane fire table** (acts as both a table and a long propane fire pit, preserving $100\%$ of the lawn).
7. **Narrow $9.0\text{ ft}$ Side Yards, Stucco-Setback Gates ($145.5\text{ lin ft}$) & Neighbor Property Line Contrast:** Both side yards are a narrow **$9.0\text{ ft}$ wide**, and neighboring lots use distinct ground surfaces so property lines are immediately visible:
   - **Neighbor Property Line Contrast:** **North Neighbor (`wy < 0.0'`): Completely Empty (Simply Bare Dirt)** outside our North fence/gate line; **South Neighbor (`wy > 50.0'`): White Rocks** outside our South fence/bed line.
   - **North Side Yard ($9.0'\text{ W} \times 43.2'\text{ L}$) & Vehicle Gate ($wx = 70.0\text{ ft}$):** Full poured concrete (cement) utility/trailer pad ($388.8\text{ sq ft}$) connected directly to the rear patio via the $3.0'$ rear-wall concrete sidewalk, with an $8.5\text{ ft}$ double-swing white vinyl gate set back $3.2\text{ ft}$ West of the NE garage face ($1.2\text{ ft}$ behind the $2.0\text{ ft}$ stone wrap).
   - **South Side Yard ($9.0'\text{ W} \times 29.7'\text{ L}$), $4.0'\text{+}$ Grass Wrap & Pedestrian Gate ($wx = 57.5\text{ ft}$):** The backyard lawn wraps **$4.0\text{ ft}\text{+}$ wide** (`wy = 43.5'–47.5'`) all the way down the $9.0\text{ ft}$ South side corridor (between the $2.0'$ South fence bed + $6"$ paver and the $2.0'$ house wall/AC screen buffer + $6"$ paver) so it looks like continuous green grass going down the entire South side to the $4.0\text{ ft}$ single-swing walk gate at $wx = 57.5\text{ ft}$ ($2.2\text{ ft}$ West of the SE stucco corner, enclosing the gas/electric meters and AC unit).
8. **Front Yard & Water-Wise $6.0\text{ ft}$ Park Strips:** Unbroken $555\text{ sq ft}$ SE front lawn framed by straight $2.0\text{ ft}$ perimeter beds and $6"$ flush pavers with **prominent $45^\circ$ chamfered corners**, 3-sided ornamental grass screen around the green utility box, specimen tree inside the front porch bed, and drip-irrigated rock/boulder/grass $6.0\text{ ft}$ street park strips.
