## 🎯 Executive Summary of Locked-In Design Decisions

1. **Continuous Open Turf (`~1,385 sq ft`, Phase 2 · 2027):** Preserves $90\%\text{–}95\%$ open turf across the Backyard ($679\text{ sq ft}$), $4.0'\text{+}$ South corridor wrap ($110\text{–}151\text{ sq ft}$), and SE Front Lawn ($555\text{ sq ft}$), continuing seamlessly under the `wx = 57.5'` South walk gate.
2. **Straight Flush Paver Mow Strip + Prominent $45^\circ$ Chamfered Corners (`~206–215 lin ft`, Phase 2 · 2027):** Home Depot **Pavestone Holland Concrete Paver** (`Charcoal Blend #21703` or `Sierra Blend #21777`) installed everywhere grass meets mulch, compacted road base, or the home (and **never** where grass meets concrete driveway, patio, or sidewalks).
3. **Western Wall `4.0'` Bed — The `5 + 4` Alternating Designer Hybrid (Phase 3 · 2028):** `5×` **`'Standing Ovation'` Serviceberry** pillars (`S1`–`S5` centered in the 5 precast panels at `wy = 5, 15, 25, 35, 45`) alternating with `4×` **Upright Evergreen Junipers** (`J1`–`J4` at the 4 interior concrete columns `wy = 10, 20, 30, 40`), under-planted with a 10-spot ribbon of purple Catmint/Russian Sage, Coneflower, and `'Karl Foerster'` Feather Reed Grasses.
4. **Compacted Road Base Network (`487 sq ft`) & 5 N–S Raised Cedar Garden Boxes (Phase 4 · 2029):** Single bulk `3/4"-minus` compacted road base delivery covering the $9.0'\times 40.0'$ North side pad inside the `wx = 70.0'` vehicle gate ($360\text{ sq ft}$), the $3.0'\times 11.0'$ rear house wall path ($33\text{ sq ft}$), and the $94\text{ sq ft}$ walkways around **5 Western Red Cedar boxes** (`N1`–`N5` @ $3.0'\text{ W}\times 6.0'\text{ L} = 90\text{ sq ft}$).
5. **House-Attached 2-Pillar Solid-Roof Heavy-Timber Patio Awning (`Option #1C` — 100% Open Sides) + Two $52"$ Lighted Ceiling Fans + L-Sectional & Propane Fire Table (Phase 5 · 2030):** Ledger-mounted at `wx = 30.0'` with strictly **2 outer West corner pillars** (`wx = 18.0', wy = 20.5' & 40.5'`), **0 house pillars**, **0 privacy walls**, a solid $1\times 6$ T&G cedar wood ceiling underneath, a matte-black standing-seam steel roof & gutter on top, and **2× $52"$ ceiling fans with built-in dimmable warm LED lights below the blades** (`0%` strobing).
6. **Narrow $9.0\text{ ft}$ Side Yards, Stucco-Setback Gates (`145.5 lin ft`) & `50/50` Neighbor Fence Split:** **Phase 1 (2026):** $57.5'$ South vinyl fence (`50%` neighbor split) + $9.0'$ South pedestrian gate assembly at `wx = 57.5'` (`100%` ours; **`$2,150` Pro / `$1,335` DIY**). **Phase 2 (2027):** $70.0'$ North vinyl fence (`50%` neighbor split) + $9.0'$ North double vehicle gate at `wx = 70.0'` (`100%` ours; **`$2,940` Pro / `$1,840` DIY**).
7. **Front Yard Norwegian Sunset® Maple (`wx = 83.0', wy = 39.5'`), Melinda's Spring Hyacinths & White Decorative Rock (Phase 2 · 2027):** Stately **Norwegian Sunset® Maple** centered in the SE Front Lawn inside a $6.0'$ octagonal paver mulch ring with 3-stage high-canopy pruning (`8.0'–9.5'` trunk attachment $\rightarrow$ `11.0'–12.0'+` outer lawn clearance ceiling), **Melinda's jewel-toned fragrant Spring Hyacinths** in front of the `'3152'` porch swing and along the continuous $2.0'$ South bed to the green box, and **white decorative rock** in both $6.0'$ street park strips and the NE side yard up to the `wx = 70.0'` North gate.
8. **5-Hydrozone Directional Sprinkler & PC Drip System (`28` Heads, `12.70 GPM`, Phase 2 · 2027):** `100%` inward-spraying perimeter `Hunter PRS40` + `MP Rotator` and `MP Side-Strip` heads (`0%` overspray onto patio, pillars, or the open AC unit at `wx = 45.0'–48.0'`) plus dedicated `30 PSI` perimeter drip and `25 PSI` garden box grid drip.
