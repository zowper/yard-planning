## 🎯 Executive Summary of Locked-In Design Decisions

1. **Final Landscape Material & Color Palette Specifications:** All planting beds and street park strips are framed with **Deep Charcoal / Onyx paver borders finished with a low-sheen color-enhancing sealer**, echoing the home's jet-black garage door, front door, shutters, and roof while sharply framing the light grey concrete sidewalk and driveway. The front yard flower beds and the $1.5'$ North border bed are filled with **Dark Espresso shredded bark mulch** (a deep umber/black-brown with zero red undertones), which harmonizes with the cool grey siding and black front door without introducing warm reddish-brown tones that would clash with the siding, while creating a dark neutral backdrop for bright porch flowers. The two $6.0'$ street park strips use **$\frac{3}{4}"\text{–}1"$ angular warm grey/taupe crushed stone**, pulling the exact weathered taupe and medium ash-grey tones out of the `3152` stone wainscot down to the street edge while staying locked in place during rain and snowplow runoff. Finally, the utility and access zone to the right of the driveway and traveling down the North side of the home uses **$\frac{3}{4}"\text{-minus}$ crushed grey limestone road base** (explicitly avoiding yellow or tan pit-run), matching the cool grey side stucco, concrete driveway, and grey/taupe stone wainscot while compacting rock-hard for trash cans and vehicles.
2. **Continuous Open Turf (`~1,435 sq ft`, Phase 2 · 2027):** Preserves $90\%\text{–}95\%$ open turf across the Backyard ($\approx 679\text{ sq ft}$), $6.0'$ South corridor wrap ($201\text{ sq ft}$, with **zero gravel anywhere on the South side**), and SE Front Lawn ($555\text{ sq ft}$), extending right up to the continuous flush Holland pavers along the entire South house wall (`wx = 30.0'–59.7'`, wrapping around the `3'×3'` AC condenser pad at `wx = 41.5'–44.5'` and returning to the wall on the East side under the notched internet/electric/gas conduits) and continuing seamlessly under the `wx = 55.5'` South walk gate.
3. **Deep Charcoal / Onyx Paver Borders + Prominent $45^\circ$ Chamfered Corners + 3-Sided NE Courtyard Frame + 4-Sided Park Strip Frames w/ `16"` Curb Step-Outs + `6"` Geotextile West Trench (`~360 lin ft` Perimeter / `~390 lin ft` Brick Equiv., Phase 2 · 2027):** Home Depot **Pavestone Holland Concrete Paver** (`#21703` Deep Charcoal / Onyx finished with a low-sheen color-enhancing sealer) framing all planting beds (including the $1.5'$ North border bed at `wy = 1.5'–2.0'` with a $45^\circ$ taper to `wx = 76.25'`), the **full 3-sided right-of-driveway road base courtyard** (along the $1.5'$ North mulch bed, city sidewalk edge at `wx = 96.2'–96.7'`, and North driveway edge at `wy = 10.5'–11.0'`), **both $6.0'$ street park strips** (full 4-sided frame with a $16"$ double-row curbside step-out at `wx = 105.37'–106.7'` + $6"$ border along the sidewalk, flared apron & N/S lot lines), and the South house foundation wall (`wx = 30.0'–59.7'`, wrapping around the Open AC Condenser pad at `wx = 41.0'–45.0'` with $\approx 4"$ cutouts for the internet [`wx = 50.0'`], electric [`wx = 51.2'`], and gas [`wx = 56.2'`] conduits; **never** where turf grass meets concrete driveway, patio, or sidewalks), with a **deepened $6"$ non-woven geotextile + $3"\text{–}4"$ compacted road-base sub-base along the $50.0'$ West wall run (`wx = 4.0'–4.5'`)**.
4. **Western `~8.0'` Wall `4.0'` Bed — The `5 + 4` Alternating Designer Hybrid on a `+3"–4"` Crowned Bed (Phase 3 · 2028):** Planted along the **`~8.0'` tall Western precast concrete privacy wall**: `5×` **`'Adirondack'` Crabapple (*Malus `'Adirondack'`*)** pillars (`S1`–`S5` centered in the 5 precast panels at `wy = 5, 15, 25, 35, 45`, `~$95/tree × 5 = $475`) alternating with `4×` **Upright Evergreen Junipers** (`J1`–`J4` at the 4 interior concrete columns `wy = 10, 20, 30, 40`), under-planted with a 10-spot ribbon of purple Catmint/Russian Sage, Coneflower, and `'Karl Foerster'` Feather Reed Grasses in Dark Espresso shredded bark mulch. Backfilled and crowned **$+3"\text{–}4"$ above the flush paver strip** in 2027 with tree/juniper root flares planted **$+2"\text{–}3"$ above bed grade** in 2028 to eliminate root rot.
5. **Continuous `3/4"-Minus` Crushed Grey Limestone Road Base Network (`~767 sq ft`) & 5 N–S Raised Cedar Garden Boxes (Phase 4 · 2029):** Single bulk `3/4"-minus` crushed grey limestone road base delivery (explicitly avoiding yellow or tan pit-run) covering the picture-framed right-of-driveway courtyard lane (`wx = 70.0'–96.2'`), the $9.0'\times 40.0'$ North side pad inside the full-width `wx = 70.0'` vehicle gate ($360\text{ sq ft}$), the $3.0'\times 11.0'$ rear house wall path ($33\text{ sq ft}$), and the walkways around **5 Western Red Cedar boxes** (`N1`–`N5` @ $3.0'\text{ W}\times 6.0'\text{ L} = 90\text{ sq ft}$).
6. **House-Attached 2-Pillar Solid-Roof Heavy-Timber Patio Awning (`Option #1C` — 100% Open Sides) + Two $52"$ Lighted Ceiling Fans + L-Sectional & Propane Fire Table (Phase 5 · 2030):** Ledger-mounted at `wx = 30.0'` with strictly **2 outer West corner pillars** (`wx = 18.0', wy = 20.5' & 40.5'`), **0 house pillars**, **0 privacy walls**, a solid $1\times 6$ T&G cedar wood ceiling underneath, a matte-black standing-seam steel roof & front gutter on top (**with its NW downspout tied into the 2027 buried $4"$ PVC drain stub-out at `wx = 18.0', wy = 20.5'`**), and **2× $52"$ ceiling fans with built-in dimmable warm LED lights below the blades** (`0%` strobing).
7. **Narrow $9.0\text{ ft}$ Side Yards, `~6.0'` White Vinyl Fences & Gates (`143.5 lin ft`), `2"` West Swale Fence Gap & `50/50` Neighbor Fence Split:** **Phase 1 (2026):** $55.5'$ South **`6.0'` tall** white vinyl fence (`50%` neighbor split, with a **$2"$ bottom-rail clearance gap over the `wx = 0.0'–6.0'` West swale** + `$0` 1-yr builder warranty West trench check) + $9.0'$ South pedestrian gate assembly at `wx = 55.5'` (`4.2'` W of SE corner, lined up against the stucco right behind the `wx = 56.0'–57.5'` gas meter; `100%` ours; **`$2,150` Pro / `$1,335` DIY**). **Phase 2 (2027):** $70.0'$ North **`6.0'` tall** white vinyl fence (`50%` neighbor split + `2"` West swale gap) + $9.0'$ full-width North double vehicle gate at `wx = 70.0'` (`3.2'` W of NE corner, lined up against the stucco right behind the `wx = 70.2'–71.2'` siding wrap and `wx = 71.2'–73.2'` stone wrap; two $5"\times 5"$ posts at `wy = 0.0'–0.42'` & `8.58'–9.0'` with zero wing panels = $8.5'$ clear opening; `100%` ours; **`$2,940` Pro / `$1,840` DIY**).
8. **Front Yard Norwegian Sunset® Maple (`wx = 83.0', wy = 39.5'`), Layered 4-Season Porch & South Border Garden (with Melinda's Hyacinth Pocket Drifts), `1.5'` North Border Bed & Planted `6.0'` Street Park Strips (Phase 2 · 2027):** Stately **Norwegian Sunset® Maple** centered in the SE Front Lawn inside a $6.0'$ octagonal paver Dark Espresso mulch ring with 3-stage high-canopy pruning (`8.0'–9.5'` trunk attachment $\rightarrow$ `11.0'–12.0'+` outer lawn clearance ceiling), a **layered 4-season front porch & South border garden** combining **`7×` Dwarf Globe Boxwoods (`'Winter Gem'`)**, **`4×` upright `'Karl Foerster'` Feather Reed Grasses**, **`8×` `'Obsidian'` burgundy & `'Caramel'` Coral Bells**, **English Lavender, Purple Catmint, Salvia & Coneflowers**, and **Melinda's jewel-toned fragrant Spring Hyacinths woven in 9 naturalistic 5-to-8 bulb pocket drifts** in front of the `'3152'` porch swing and along the continuous $2.0'$ South bed to the green box, a **$1.5'$ North border bed (`wx = 78.0'–96.7'` w/ $45^\circ$ taper to `wx = 76.5'`)** in Dark Espresso mulch with `4×` `'Karl Foerster'` grasses + `4×` purple Catmint, and **two $6.0'$ street park strips** in $\frac{3}{4}"\text{–}1"$ angular warm grey/taupe crushed stone framed by Deep Charcoal/Onyx pavers (`16"` curb step-out) with `'Karl Foerster'` grasses, purple Catmint/Lavender, and South Hyacinth accents.
9. **5-Hydrozone Directional Sprinkler & PC Drip System (`28` Heads, `13.18 GPM` w/ Under-Driveway Conduit Extension) + Buried `4"` Smooth-Wall PVC Downspout & West Catch-Basin Stormwater Drainage (`+$280 DIY` / `+$650 Pro`, Phase 2 · 2027):** `100%` inward-spraying perimeter `Hunter PRS40` + `MP Rotator` and `MP Side-Strip` heads (with **`Hunter PRS40-CV` check-valve bodies** on low West heads `B1, B6, B8, B9, B11` and `0%` overspray onto patio, pillars, or the open AC unit at `wx = 41.5'–44.5'`), **Zone 4 (`30 PSI` PC Drip) extended under the sidewalk and through the existing builder under-driveway PVC conduit (`wx ≈ 85'`)** to irrigate the $1.5'$ North border bed and both $6.0'$ street park strips, plus **`~140 lin ft` of buried $4"$ smooth-wall PVC (`SDR-35`/`Sch 40`) drain lines** replacing the temporary black corrugated flex pipes on the NW (`wx=30', wy=9'`) & SW (`wx=30', wy=41'`) rear downspouts, tied into **$2\times$ $9"\times 9"$ West lawn catch basins (`wx=4.5', wy=12' & 38'`)** and sloped East to front pop-up emitters (`wx ≈ 95'`). *(See [`docs/04-irrigation-and-drainage.md`](./docs/04-irrigation-and-drainage.md) for all 3 Oct 2026 rain-pooling photos and the 7-point stormwater engineering table).*
